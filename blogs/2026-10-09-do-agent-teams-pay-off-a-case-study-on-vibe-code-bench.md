We ran GPT 6 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5 on Vibe Code Bench as single agents and as teams, at medium and max reasoning effort. Teams cost 1.8 to 5.1 times as much, and only for Sol at medium effort was the difference in score statistically significant. Whether a team is worth it depends on the model, task, and budget: a team can boost performance, but higher reasoning effort is an alternative worth weighing.
